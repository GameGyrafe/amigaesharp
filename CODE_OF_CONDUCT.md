# Code of conduct

E# is a small hobby-scale project about a machine many of us love. We want it to be a friendly place to learn and build things.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners and with people who know the Amiga better or worse than you do.
- Assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. Say what you tried and what happened.
- Respect other people's work and the licences it comes with.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Sharing pirated software, including Kickstart ROMs and commercial Amiga software, in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may restrict or remove people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, message a maintainer through GitHub (the owners of the [GameGyrafe](https://github.com/GameGyrafe) organisation). Reports are treated privately where possible.

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and in any space where someone represents the project.
